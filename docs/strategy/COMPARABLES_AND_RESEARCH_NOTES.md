## Notes

- Instagram is likely best for a social media presence, followed by TikTok.
- Aim for a personal feel with posts without coming off as trendy.
- Does the Pocket want to be seen as "fun"? Does it want to avoid being seen as "fun"? *(Open question for the brand interview.)*
- Use atmosphere as storytelling.
- Lean into seasonal dishes/wines with seasonal campaigns and promotions.
- Chef stories, behind-the-scenes content, and professional photography and video to create a refined but approachable presence.
- Social media should communicate an entire experience, not just individual dishes:
  - What is changing seasonally
  - Who is creating the experience
  - What the patio and restaurant feel like
  - How food and wine come together
  - Why locals should return
  - Why visitors should choose The Pocket
  - What special events or gatherings are possible

## Guidance from the professor (as recorded by the team)

- A **dual-segment** marketing strategy is the way to go: tourists and locals.
- **Management must own the brand.** The strategy needs to incorporate management's knowledge and input about the brand, so the assistant must be able to get at that key knowledge by asking the right questions and interpreting the answers correctly.
- The strategy must include **who owns what**: responsibilities clearly defined, including the responsibilities of the AI tool.

How the AI Marketing Assistant reflects this guidance:

| Guidance | In the application |
|---|---|
| Dual segment | Every draft and campaign is aimed at locals, tourists, or both, with different reasons to visit but one brand. |
| Management owns the brand | Brand rules come from management interviews and only an administrator can approve them. AI suggestions stay proposals. |
| Clear ownership, including the AI's | Roles (administrator, approver, contributor, viewer) are enforced by the server. The AI drafts, organizes and suggests; it cannot approve, publish or change the brand. |
