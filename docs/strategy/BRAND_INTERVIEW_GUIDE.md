# The Pocket — Brand Interview Guide

**For:** the capstone team interviewing The Pocket's management (Chef Mike, GM Elliot, and ownership where appropriate).
**Purpose:** capture management's own view of the brand so the marketing strategy and the AI Marketing Assistant reflect it. Management owns the brand. Nothing said in this interview becomes an official brand rule until management approves the exact wording afterwards.
**Length:** about 60 minutes per person. Sections 1–5 matter most; if time runs short, skip to section 8.
**Status:** draft question set, 2026-11-13. The same questions (same IDs) are used by the in-app interview feature.

---

## Before the interview

1. **Interview each manager separately if possible.** Differences between their answers are useful, not a problem. The team records them; it does not resolve them.
2. **Ask permission for two things** and note the answer:
   - May we record or take detailed notes?
   - May your answers be entered into the AI Marketing Assistant, which sends text to Anthropic's Claude service to draft summaries? (If no, answers are typed in by hand and kept out of AI processing.)
3. **Bring:** the strategy summary ([MARKETING_STRATEGY.md](MARKETING_STRATEGY.md)) as a one-page handout, this guide, and a way to take notes.
4. **Do not lead.** Ask the open question first; use the follow-ups only if the answer is short. Avoid suggesting the team's positioning before hearing theirs.

## How to record answers

For each question, write down:
- The question ID (for example `B2`).
- **Their words**, as close to verbatim as possible, especially memorable phrases.
- Anything they said *must* or *must never* be said.
- Facts that will change (menu items, events, hours) and **who verifies them**.

## 1. The Pocket in their words (identity)

| ID | Question | Follow-ups if needed |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | How would you describe The Pocket to someone who has never been? | What would you want them to remember afterwards? |
| A2 | What makes The Pocket different from other upscale restaurants in Carmel and Monterey? | Which of those would a guest notice first? |
| A3 | If The Pocket were a person, how would you describe their personality? | Three words you would use, and three you would never want used. |
| A4 | What is the story behind The Pocket: why it exists and what you are proud of? | Is any of that story private or not for marketing? |

## 2. Tone and voice

| ID | Question | Follow-ups if needed |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | When The Pocket speaks publicly (social media, website, emails), how should it sound? | Formal or conversational? Warm, witty, understated? |
| B2 | Should The Pocket be seen as "fun"? Or is that something to avoid? | What does "fun" look like that would or would not fit? |
| B3 | Show or describe a restaurant post or ad you liked, and one you disliked. Why? | What made the bad one feel wrong? |
| B4 | Are there words, phrases, emojis or styles that should never appear in The Pocket's marketing? | Words like "luxury", "exclusive", "best in Carmel"? |

## 3. Guests: locals and visitors

| ID | Question | Follow-ups if needed |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Who are your regulars? What brings them back? | What would make them come back more often? |
| C2 | Who are your visitors? How do they usually find you today? | Inn guests, hotels, concierges, Google, word of mouth? |
| C3 | What should a local hear from The Pocket that a tourist doesn't need to, and the reverse? | Is there anything you would say to only one group? |
| C4 | What promise does The Pocket make to every guest? | What would a broken promise look like? |

## 4. What makes the experience

| ID | Question | Follow-ups if needed |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | How should the covered, heated patio be described? | Seasons or weather where it shines? Anything to avoid claiming? |
| D2 | How does the menu change, and how far ahead do you know what's coming? | Who confirms a dish is available before it is promoted? |
| D3 | What role do the chef and pastry program play in the brand? | Is the chef comfortable appearing in photos, video or short interviews? |
| D4 | How important is wine to The Pocket's identity? | Wine dinners, pairings, a sommelier story? |
| D5 | What does personal hospitality mean at The Pocket? | A guest story that captures it, if you're comfortable sharing? |
| D6 | How do the Inn and the restaurant relate, and how should that be described? | Who handles referrals between them? |

## 5. Events and offers

| ID | Question | Follow-ups if needed |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | What private events or special occasions do you want more of? Which do you not want? | Group sizes, dates or occasions that work best? |
| E2 | How do you feel about promotions and discounts? | Anything that would cheapen the brand? Anything acceptable (wine dinner pricing, seasonal menus)? |
| E3 | What is coming up in the next three months that should be promoted? | Who confirms dates, prices and capacity? |

## 6. Boundaries and approvals

| ID | Question | Follow-ups if needed |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | What should marketing never say or show? | Claims, comparisons with competitors, guests in photos, prices? |
| F2 | Who must approve what before it goes public? | Does food need the chef's check? Does anything need ownership's sign-off? |
| F3 | Are you comfortable with either manager approving a post they wrote themselves? | Should anything always need a second person? |
| F4 | What should the AI assistant never do, even as a draft? | Topics it should stay away from? |

## 7. Goals and measuring success

| ID | Question | Follow-ups if needed |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Six months from now, what would make you say marketing is working? | More locals returning, more visitors, more events, less of your time? |
| G2 | How much time per week can someone realistically give to marketing, and who? | Is there a natural marketing lead on staff? |
| G3 | What information do you already have about reservations and where guests come from? | Toast or Tock reports you could share in aggregate? |

## 8. Closing

| ID | Question |
|---|---|
| H1 | Is there anything about The Pocket we haven't asked that marketing must get right? |
| H2 | Who else should we talk to (ownership, kitchen, front of house)? |

## After the interview: from answers to approved brand rules

1. **Type up the notes** within a day, using the question IDs.
2. **Draft proposed brand rules.** Each rule is one clear statement plus the quote that supports it.
   - Example. *Proposed rule:* "Describe the patio as covered and heated; do not call it outdoor-only or seasonal." *Support (D1, Elliot):* "…"
   - In the app, the AI can draft these from the notes, but every one is saved as a **proposal**, never an approved rule.
3. **Flag disagreements.** If Mike and Elliot answered differently, list both versions side by side. Do not pick one.
4. **Management reviews** each proposed rule and either approves the exact wording, edits it, or rejects it. Only an administrator can approve. Approved rules become the brand core the assistant always uses.
5. **Facts are separate.** Menu items, hours, events and prices are recorded as facts with the person who verified them and when they expire, not as brand rules.

| Interview section | Becomes |
|---|---|

| 7 (goals) | KPI targets, kept separate from measured results |
